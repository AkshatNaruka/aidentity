# Music producer AI Agent Setup Playbook (Individual & Enterprise)

**Definition:** The **Music producer AI Agent Playbook** configures any coding agent (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Gemini CLI, OpenCode, or GitHub Copilot) for lyrics, song structure, mastering, album concepts and release assets. It installs 11 SHA-256 security-verified skills in the Individual profile and 15 skills plus 4 runtime guardrails in the Enterprise profile.

## Quick Commands

```bash
# Individual setup (11 verified skills)
npx aidentity setup music-producer --profile individual

# Enterprise setup (15 verified skills + supply-chain & security hooks)
npx aidentity setup music-producer --profile enterprise
```

## Individual vs. Enterprise Comparison

| Component | Individual Profile | Enterprise Profile |

| Verified Skills | 11 core skills | 15 skills (+4 security/audit skills) |
| Coding Rules | 0 rule files | 0 rule files |
| Subagents | 2 subagents | 3 subagents |

| Safety Hooks | hooks/scan-secrets.js, hooks/block-dangerous.js | hooks/scan-secrets.js, hooks/block-dangerous.js, hooks/format-on-save.js, hooks/test-on-stop.js |

## Verified Skills Included

- **lw-song-intent** (`jtydhr88-lyric-writing-skills/lw-song-intent`, MIT, SHA-256 ``): The gate you must pass before writing a single line (动笔前必填). Five questions that force an actual choice - which kind of song this is (attitudinal, situational, narrative, or expository, and why the default is not narrative), the one thing it says, who it is said to, at what moment, and the one photographable object that runs through it - with the failure mode each question catches, the downstream choices the song type fixes (person, voice, repetition budget), how to tell a real answer from a filled-in one, and why this gate exists at all (AI-written lyrics are not bad at words, they are written by nobody). Use when starting any lyric, when a draft feels generic but you cannot say why, when every song comes out as a story, when a song is trying to say two things, or when LYR-LINT check 1 fails. 立意、动笔前必填、歌的类型、态度型、情境型、叙事型、说理型、只说一件事、对谁说、锚定物件、重复预算、写不出来、太泛。
- **lw-structure** (`jtydhr88-lyric-writing-skills/lw-structure`, MIT, SHA-256 ``): The structural layer of a lyric (词的结构层) - what each section is for and what it must not say, which song form to pick, where the title lands and how often it comes back, and how line counts, line lengths and rhyme placement make a section feel finished or still owing something. Covers verse / pre-chorus / chorus / bridge / refrain functions, central versus developmental sections, AABA and verse-chorus and verse-refrain forms, the five structural parameters (balance, pace, flow, closure, closure type), the five hook-placement strategies, and the deliberate asymmetry that keeps a lyric from sounding machine-made. Use when laying out sections before writing a line, when a chorus does not feel like an arrival, when a bridge sounds like one more verse, when every section came out the same length, when deciding where the title goes, or when LYR-LINT check 3, 4 or 10 fails. 段落结构、曲式、副歌、bridge、hook、标题位置、句式、不对称、段落功能、AABA。
- **lw-narrative** (`jtydhr88-lyric-writing-skills/lw-narrative`, MIT, SHA-256 ``): Who is speaking, when they are speaking, and how much they are allowed to know (歌词的叙事层). The three narrative fields every lyric must fill - point of view, tense anchor, and the one thing deliberately left unsaid - with the cost of each person, the risk of mixing pronouns mid-song and the few shifts that actually work, how present/past/future change the listener's distance, why a fixed moment limits what the speaker can know (write the symptom, not the conclusion), why a completed emotional arc (hurt - peace - blessing) is a machine fingerprint, and how a story lyric advances through scene, turn and cost. Use when a draft has no speaker, when you cannot tell who is being addressed, when pronouns drift between sections, when the timeline is half memory and half now, when a lyric resolves everything and leaves nothing hanging, when writing a story song, or when LYR-LINT check 5 or 6 fails. 视角、人称、时间、时态、留白、不解决、叙事、故事型歌词、没有说话人、谁在说话、情绪走完整。

Object writing and the seven senses, the ten-minute timed practice, why abstract nouns are the most direct AI fingerprint and how to build the forbidden list, how to check that a lyric is not all sight, why a set of images must belong to one physical world instead of being piled up at random, simile versus metaphor and how to spot and replace a dead one, and how one object recurs with a shifting meaning. Use when a lyric feels vague or generic but the words look fine, when every noun in a draft is a concept, when you cannot find images, when images are pretty but unrelated, when a metaphor sounds borrowed, or when LYR-LINT check 2 fails. 意象、具体名词、抽象词、object writing、物体写作、五感、七感、比喻、明喻、暗喻、通感、陈词、死喻、词太泛、找不到意象。
- **lw-english** (`jtydhr88-lyric-writing-skills/lw-english`, MIT, SHA-256 ``): The English language layer for lyric writing (英文词的重音与节拍对齐). Use when writing or checking English lyrics, fitting English words to a melody, when a sung line sounds mispronounced, when counting syllables, or when a line will not fit the bar. 英文作词、英文歌词、重音、节拍对齐、音节数、prosody、phrasing、气口、重音落错、唱不进小节。
