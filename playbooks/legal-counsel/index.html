Frequently Asked Questions — Legal counsel AI Setup

How do I set up Claude Code or Cursor for a legal counsel?

Run npx aidentity setup legal-counsel --profile individual --agent claude (or --agent cursor). The CLI verifies SHA-256 digests against security.json and writes the skills, coding rules, subagents, and hooks to your agent directory.

What is the difference between the Individual and Enterprise legal counsel setup?

The Individual profile installs 9 core skills and essential secret/command safety hooks. The Enterprise profile installs 11 skills — adding Trail of Bits supply-chain risk auditing, agentic actions auditing, and security hardening — plus format-on-save and test-on-stop hooks.

Are all skills in the Legal counsel playbook security-verified?

Yes. Every skill in this playbook is scanned by scripts/scan-skills-security.js for hidden Unicode, hardcoded credentials, destructive commands, and prompt injection, with SHA-256 hashes published at security.json.
