AI SEO, AEO & Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Course

Master traditional technical SEO alongside AI Search Optimization (AEO/GEO) so your product is indexed by Google and cited or selected as the default by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini.

What You Will Learn & Automate

  • Structure pages with 40–60 word extractable answer blocks, comparison tables, and JSON-LD schema.

How do I set up Claude Code or Cursor for a seo specialist?

Run npx aidentity setup seo-specialist --profile individual --agent claude (or --agent cursor). The CLI verifies SHA-256 digests against security.json and writes the skills, coding rules, subagents, and hooks to your agent directory.

What is the difference between the Individual and Enterprise seo specialist setup?

The Individual profile installs 12 core skills and essential secret/command safety hooks. The Enterprise profile installs 16 skills, adding Trail of Bits supply-chain risk auditing, agentic actions auditing, and security hardening, plus format-on-save and test-on-stop hooks.

Are all skills in the SEO specialist playbook security-verified?

Yes. Every skill in this playbook is scanned by scripts/scan-skills-security.js for hidden Unicode, hardcoded credentials, destructive commands, and prompt injection, with SHA-256 hashes published at security.json.
